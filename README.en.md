## About

I am a software engineer based in Brasília, Brazil, with over 4 years of practical experience building digital products from zero to deployment. I work across mobile engineering with Flutter and React Native with releases on Google Play and the App Store, modern web platforms with Next.js and TypeScript, and decoupled backend services in Node.js with Prisma and relational databases (MySQL and PostgreSQL).

Currently at **Sistema Boss** (the technology unit behind Agência Sisters and Movimento Vida 360), managing mobile release cycles with Expo/EAS and migrating legacy monoliths into decoupled Node.js services. I am also co-founder and tech lead at **Exímia Tech**, architecting SaaS solutions and mentoring junior engineering squads.
